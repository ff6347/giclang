Adapted from [Fabian's grid of rectangles sketch](https://snippets.qawsed.site/p5js/loop/grid-of-rects/sketch.js), this 9 by 9 grid turns each square's four corners around its center with sine and cosine. The rotation angle changes by column, from 0 to 80 degrees. Each corner uses the square's diagonal radius and a 45-degree base angle plus its column rotation, expressing the shape directly in polar coordinates. Default black outlines remain visible.

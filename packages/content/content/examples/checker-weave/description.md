Adapted from [Fabian's weave-pattern sketch](https://snippets.qawsed.site/p5js/loop/weave-pattern/index.js), this 5 by 5 checkerboard alternates cells of three horizontal lines with cells of three vertical lines. The cell index determines both its position and orientation, while one-pixel black strokes keep the weave crisp.

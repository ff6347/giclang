Based on [Fabian's nested-loop bubbles sketch](https://snippets.qawsed.site/p5js/loop/nested-loops-bubbles/index.js), nested repeats place 225 circles on a regular grid. Their radii scale from the original 25–70 pixel diameters to GIC radii of 3.125–8.75; translucent OKLCH hues range from 30 to 230. Black outlines are retained at quarter width to match the scaled source. The original 400-pixel square grid is scaled to fit the 100-pixel GIC canvas.

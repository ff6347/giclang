Adapted from [Fabian's dancing triangles sketch](https://snippets.qawsed.site/p5js/loop/dancing-triangles/index.js), this composition places 100 irregular triangles across a dark square. A randomly rotated triangle uses irregular 100–120 degree gaps; a shared x/y jitter is rotated along with each vertex. Its radius follows the source's reciprocal random scale, giving radii from 4 to 20, and translucent pastel OKLCH fills retain the full 0–100 alpha range. Default black outlines remain. The original tall format is composed for GIC's square canvas.

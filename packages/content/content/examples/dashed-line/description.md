Based on [Fabian's dashed-line sketch](https://snippets.qawsed.site/p5js/loop/dashed-line/index.js), ten short line segments form a 45-degree diagonal dash pattern. Their start points follow the original translated and rotated coordinates, stepping five pixels of dash and five pixels of gap in local space.

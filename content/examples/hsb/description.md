Adapted from [HSB](https://github.com/ff6347/gestalten-in-code/tree/e8b8f8446c13c01494ccfd1e92ce2bb1638daf66/color/p5js/hsb), created by [Fabian Morón Zirfas](http://fabianmoronzirfas.me).

Three equal vertical fields hold saturation and brightness constant while shifting hue by 120 degrees. The study isolates hue contrast through fixed CSS colors and a simple triptych structure.

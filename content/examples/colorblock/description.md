Adapted from [Colorblock](https://github.com/ff6347/gestalten-in-code/tree/e8b8f8446c13c01494ccfd1e92ce2bb1638daf66/color/p5js/colorblock), created by [Julia Hilt](https://github.com/piixelcat).

Three touching blocks move through a warm monochromatic range, with the center color fixed halfway between the outer hues. Their shared edges turn separate rectangles into one compact study of color and proximity.

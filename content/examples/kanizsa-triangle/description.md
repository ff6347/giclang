Adapted from [Kanizsa's Triangle](https://github.com/ff6347/gestalten-in-code/tree/e8b8f8446c13c01494ccfd1e92ce2bb1638daf66/form/2d/processing/Kanizsa_s_Triangle), created by [Fabian Morón Zirfas](http://fabianmoronzirfas.me).

Three dark circular inducers are opened toward the same empty space, making a white triangle appear without drawing its edges. The composition demonstrates how closure turns carefully placed gaps into a convincing figure.

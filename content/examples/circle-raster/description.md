Adapted from [Circle Raster](https://github.com/ff6347/gestalten-in-code/tree/e8b8f8446c13c01494ccfd1e92ce2bb1638daf66/repeat/p5js/circle-raster), created by [Fabian Morón Zirfas](http://fabianmoronzirfas.me).

A uniform field of circles establishes strong similarity and order, while one deterministically selected circle doubles in size. That single change makes the larger mark immediately salient without disturbing the grid.
